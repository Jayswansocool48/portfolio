Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This site collects nothing about you. It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts, no forms and no accounts. It sets no cookies.

The fonts and every script load from this site itself, so your browser talks to no other company while you read it.

If you switch between light and dark, or pick a filter on the Work list, your browser remembers that choice in its own local storage. That choice stays on your device and never reaches me. Clear your browser’s site data to erase it.

The host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs such as your IP address. GitHub’s own privacy statement covers those logs.

Questions or a deletion request: email Iron Lantern Studios at the address in the footer of the home page. I hold no data about you to delete, and I’ll confirm that in writing if you ask.
