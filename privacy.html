Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This site collects nothing about you. It has no analytics, no ads, no tracking scripts and no accounts, and it sets no cookies. The fonts and every script load from this site itself, so your browser talks to no other company while you read it.

The contact form

The form on the Contact page never sends anything to this site. It opens your own email app with your message filled in. When you send it, I receive a normal email, and I keep it only as long as I need to reply. Ask me to delete it any time.

Buying

When you press a buy button here, a checkout page from my payment provider opens. That provider collects your payment details and your email address so it can send you the download and a receipt, under its own privacy policy. I see your email and what you bought. I never see your card.

If you buy on Steam, itch.io, Google Play, the App Store, Fab or Roblox instead, that store handles your details under its own policy.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may keep standard server logs such as your IP address. GitHub’s privacy statement covers those logs.

Your data

Email the address on the Contact page to ask what I hold about you or to have it deleted. I’ll answer within 30 days.
