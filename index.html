After Hours

You sit at a computer after everyone has gone home. You check email, open the browser, finish the job. Behind you, the room keeps changing.

Style
PSX-era, low to medium poly
Structure
Chapters, one person each
Now building
Chapter 1, the house

The desktop is the game

You play almost the whole game at a desk. The computer in front of you runs a full in-game operating system, with a browser, a mail client and files you can dig through. You use it to do the work each chapter gives you.

The horror stays out of the monitor. It happens in the room: a door you closed now stands open, a shape sits where a chair used to be. You turn around when you choose to, and you choose less often as the night goes on.

Anything you can touch shows its name and a key the moment you look at it, so you always know what you can do and never know what you should.
