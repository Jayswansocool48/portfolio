After Hours

You stay late at a computer after everyone else goes home. You answer email and finish your work, and each time you turn around, something in the room has moved.

Style
PSX-era, low to medium poly
Structure
Chapters, one person each
Now building
Chapter 1, the house

The desktop is the game

You spend almost the whole game at a desk. The computer in front of you runs a full in-game operating system with a browser, a mail client and files to dig through, and you use it to finish each chapter’s work.

The scares come from the room around you. You shut the door an hour ago and now it hangs open. Someone sits in the chair you walked past earlier. You decide when to turn around, and the later it gets, the longer you put it off.

Look at anything you can use and you’ll see its name and the key to press, like E for the desk lamp.
